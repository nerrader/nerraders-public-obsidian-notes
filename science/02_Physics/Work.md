**Work** (measured in Joules, $J$) is the process of transferring energy by applying a force to move an object over a distance.
$$W = F \cdot d$$

- **$F$** (Force): How hard you push/pull in Newtons.
- **$d$** ([[Displacement]]): How far the object moved in meters.

This means even if you push an extremely heavy object as hard as you can but it didn't move, physics still says you did no work at all, as displacement is zero.
