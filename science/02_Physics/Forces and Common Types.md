Forces are a push or pull resulting from interaction between objects, it is an action that can change an object's shape, velocity, or pressure.

It is a [[00_SI Units#Vector Quantities (Magnitude + Direction)|vector quantity]], measured using Newtons (N).

## The Four Fundamental Forces

There are four fundamental forces to which every other force derives from, and are responsible for shaping the world that we inhibit in.

These forces include:
- [[Gravity]]
- [[Electromagnetism]]
- [[Weak Nuclear Force]]
- [[Strong Nuclear Force]]
## Types of Forces

Forces are generally categorized into two main types based on interaction.

- **Contact Forces**: These occur when objects physically touch. Examples for this type of force include friction, normal force, tension, air resistance, and applied force.
  
- **Non-Contact (Field) Forces**: These act over distances without physical contact. Examples of this type of force include all of the fundamental forces.
## List of Common Forces

All these other common **emergent** forces are just derived from the four fundamental forces of nature.

| Force                                                | Symbol       | Description                                |

| [[Friction]]                                         | $f$          | Resistive force opposing motion on land    |
| Drag                                                 | $D$          | Resistive force opposing motion in fluids  |
| Tension                                              | $T$          | Force transmitted through string or wire   |
