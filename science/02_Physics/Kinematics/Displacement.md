Displacement is the change in position of an object. It is the distance between it's initial position and its final position. Unlike distance, which measures its total path length travelled.

Displacement is also a [[Types of Quantities#Vector Quantities|vector quantity]], meaning that it measures magnitude and direction.

This means that if you travel 10 kilometers east and 10 kilometers back west:
- Your total distance will be 20km.
- While your total displacement is zero, as there is no distance between your initial position and your final position, since you ended up at the same place you started.
