Acceleration is the concept defined as the rate of change of velocity over time.
- Acceleration is a [[Scalar and Vector Quantities#Vector Quantities (Magnitude + Direction) | vector quantity]], meaning an object can accelerate and decelerate if speed changes.
- The SI Unit is $m/s^2$ because it is defined as change of velocity (m/s) over time (s)
