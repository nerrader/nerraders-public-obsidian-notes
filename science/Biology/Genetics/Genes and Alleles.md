Genes are sequences or segments of DNA. These contain instructions for cells for specific body functions or specific traits. ~~These genes control everything from hair color to instructions for the stomach on how to process food.~~

- Genes come in pairs, one from each parent.
- Genes are located in [[Chromosomes]].
- **Not all genes are active in every cell.** Different cells turn different genes on or off depending on their function. A heart cell and a skin cell have the same DNA but can use different genes to make different proteins.

> [!IMPORTANT] Genes DO NOT create traits directly, or by themselves.
> 
> This is a common textbook simplification. Genes do not control traits themselves, or provide instructions on how certain functions in the body are carried out.
> 
> They instead provide the instructions to create proteins in our body, which then control the traits like eye color, and also specific instructions like how to keep the heart pumping, or how to digest food in the stomach.
## Alleles

Alleles are different variations of the same gene which produce different variations of that gene trait. This is also the reason why genetic diversity exists.

There are also two types of alleles: the dominant allele (A), and the recessive allele (a). If there is a dominant allele, it is usually expressed and will mask/hide the trait of the recessive allele. However, there is a way of codominance where both traits or alleles are expressed at the same time.

**Heterozygous** alleles mean that the allele pair has both a dominant and recessive gene (Aa)
**Homogenous** alleles mean that the allele pair has the same type. (aa or AA)
## Genotypes and Phenotypes

- Genotypes describe the individual's genetic makeup, or the set of alleles it carries. This represents the underlying DNA sequence.
- Phenotypes describe the individual's overall observable characteristics, which is affected by its genotype and the environment.
  
A good example of this are cats, different genotypes can produce different phenotypes, as one cat could have straight ears, and another cat could have curly ears due to differences in their genes for ear shapes
