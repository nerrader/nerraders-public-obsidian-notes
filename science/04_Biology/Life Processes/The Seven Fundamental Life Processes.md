The seven life processes are the essential activities all organisms perform to maintain life, grow and reproduce. It is only classified as an organism, if it is capable of carrying out all seven life processes independently.

Here are the seven life processes, in no particular order:

- [[Sensitivity]]
- [[Respiration]]
- [[Reproduction]]
- [[Excretion]]
- [[Growth]]
- [[Movement]]
- [[Nutrition]]

> [!INFO] Viruses are not Organisms
> 
> This goes back to what we just discussed, [[Viruses]] **do not independantly** carry out **any** of the life processes mentioned, with the exception of being able to reproduce only inside a host cell.
