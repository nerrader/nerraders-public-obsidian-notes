# Viruses

- Viruses are a type of microscopic infectious agent or [[Pathogens|pathogen]] that can replicate inside cells of living things (aka a host). Viruses are much smaller than bacteria.

- Viruses do not have any cell walls, mitochondria, nucleus, and so they cannot live without being inside a living cell. If the host dies, the virus also dies along with them.

- Viruses cause various diseases in multiple organisms like plants, animals and humans by hijacking the cellular machinery to replicate more copies of the virus and spread.

> [!INFO] Viruses are NOT organisms
> 
> Unlike bacteria, viruses are not considered organisms or living creatures, because it cannot perform any of [[The Seven Fundamental Life Processes]] by itself.
> 
> - It moves passively via water, air or other organisms.
> - It can only reproduce inside a host cell.
> - It does not grow, respond to any stimuli, does not excrete anything.
>   
> You get the point.

- Unlike bacteria, viruses are not living creatures, therefore they are not in any kingdom.
