Chromosomes are a structure that contain a **single, long molecule of DNA**, wrapped in proteins called histones, which allow it to fit in a compact cell.

- In human body cells, chromosomes normally come in pairs, so the number of chromosomes in a normal body is always an even number (23 pairs = 46 chromosomes).
- These are usually located in the nucleus of animal and plant cells.

> [!NOTE] Gametes in Mammals
> 
> For mammals, their gametes (like sperm and egg), usually only have half the amount of chromosomes.
> 
> Sperm = 23 chromosomes
> Egg = 23 chromosomes
> 
> So that when they fuse, they get a full pair.
