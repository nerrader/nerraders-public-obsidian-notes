**DNA**, short for deoxyribonucleic acid, is a complex molecule that carries genetic instructions for the general life processes of all known organisms and many viruses. 

- It contains the hereditary material (genes that shape our characteristic traits) passed from the individual's parents during reproduction.
- DNA is mostly always found in the nucleus of animal and plant cells (where it is called nuclear DNA). Sometimes, it is also found in the mitochondria of some organisms.
## Structure

DNA is structured like a **double helix**.
There are four bases found in DNA:

- **A** = Adenine
- **T** = Thymine
- **C** = Cytosine
- **G** = Guanine

Remember that A connects with T, and C connects with G.
