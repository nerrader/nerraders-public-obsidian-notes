> [!ABSTRACT]
>
> - The **Carbon Cycle** shows the process of recycling carbon.
> - It is the process by which carbon atoms, move through the Earth’s atmosphere, oceans, soil, and living organisms.
> - Because only a tiny number of atoms reach the Earth from space, our planet is called a closed system. This means the Earth does not gain or lose carbon. But carbon does move constantly.

## System Dynamics

Carbon moves between areas of earth via specific chemical and biological processes.

| **Process**        | **Direction**                          |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------- |
| **Photosynthesis** | Atmosphere $\rightarrow$ Organism      |
| **Respiration**    | Organism $\rightarrow$ Atmosphere      |
| **Decomposition**  | Organism $\rightarrow$ Soil/Atmosphere |
| **Combustion**     | Fossil Fuels $\rightarrow$ Atmosphere  |
| **Ocean Exchange** | Atmosphere $\leftrightarrow$ Ocean     |

There are two different methods that the Earth processes, transforms, and transports carbon.

### Biological Path

This path focuses on how carbon moves on living systems via regulated enzymatic reactions. Plants and algae fix carbon into organic molecules. When organisms respire or die, this carbon is returned to the environment.

- **Photosynthesis:** $6CO_2 + 6H_2O \rightarrow C_6H_{12}O_6 + 6O_2$
- **Respiration:** $C_6H_{12}O_6 + 6O_2 \rightarrow 6CO_2 + 6H_2O$
- **Decomposition:** Essential for nutrient recycling; facilitated by [[Decomposers]].

### Chemical Path

This path focuses on the through abiotic systems through thermodynamic and physical reactions, and the transformation of carbon compounds and their state of matter

- **Combustion:** The rapid oxidation of carbon-based fuels.
    - Equation: $Fuel + O_2 \rightarrow CO_2 + H_2O + Energy$
- **Ocean Acidification:** The chemical reaction of $CO_2$ dissolving in seawater to form carbonic acid ($H_2CO_3$).
