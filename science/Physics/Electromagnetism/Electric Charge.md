An electric charge is a fundamental property of matter that allows particles to electrically interact with one another.

There are two types of electric charges: positive and negative.
Like charges repel, opposite attract.
Example: positive repels positive, positive attracts negative.

An object usually becomes charged, through the transfer of electrons. Losing electrons means it is positively charged and vice versa.

The [[SI Units|SI Unit]] for electric charge is the Columb (C), which is equivalent to $6.24 \times 10^{18}$
