Voltage is just electrical potential difference, kind of the reason why we need GND.

For some reason electrons can't just flow, it needs a reference point to go down to or something, so usually it is GND (0V), though it can be anything

Things going from 12V to 5V (which is basically just 7V -> GND) is real, and will flow current, if it is a closed loop.

It's apparently also equivalent to the amount of energy a battery puts on each Coulomb, so it can also be measured as $V = E/Q$, where E = energy used over time in seconds, which is just $E = Pt$

Oh yeah voltage is the same in parallel circuits but different in series circuits.
