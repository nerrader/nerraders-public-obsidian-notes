Electrostatics explores static electricity.

Pretty much, if you've rubbed your hair on a balloon, or on any surface actually. That surface then gets negatively charged, while your hair gets positively charged. The electrons got transferred from your hair to the surface/object.

So then that object will attract and pull on your hair.

You know how sometimes when you touch a metal object in a mall or something, then you feel that zap or shock?

Repeated contact of the floor from your shoe transfers the electrons to your shoe, which then travelled up your body into your hands, and then it does electric discharge from your body to the metal, creating this zap feeling.
