Electricity is the behavior of electric charges. They can come in some different forms:
- Static electricity
- Electric circuits
- Electric fields

Also, note that electricity is not just electrons moving and then boom electric current, it's way more than that.

And it's not even about the electrons moving, its usually the electrons that create an electric field which is the one that actually powers components and stuff.
