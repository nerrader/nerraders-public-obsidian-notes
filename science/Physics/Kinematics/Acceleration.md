Acceleration is the concept defined as the rate of change of [[Velocity]] over time.
- Acceleration is a [[Types of Quantities#Vector Quantities|vector quantity]], meaning an object can accelerate and decelerate if speed changes.
- The SI Unit is $m/s^2$ because it is defined as change of velocity (m/s) over time (s)
