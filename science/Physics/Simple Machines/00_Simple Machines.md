Simple machines are basic mechanical devices that change the magnitude or direction of force to make [[Work]] easier.

Some examples of simple machines include:
- [[Levers]]
- [[Pulleys]]
- [[Inclined Planes]]
- [[Wedges]]
- [[Screws]]
- [[Wheel and Axle]]

## Mechanical Advantage

Remember how in the previous definition I said that it modifies force to make work easier? This is how.

Mechanical advantage describes how much a machine multiplies the force you apply into a bigger force. The equation for mechanical advantage is:$$MA = \frac{E_{out}}{E_{in}}$$Simple machines achieve mechanical advantage by trading force for distance. You apply more force over a greater distance to exert an overall magnified, bigger force.

Different simple machines achieve mechanical advantage in different ways.
