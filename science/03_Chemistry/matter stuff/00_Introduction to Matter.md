Matter is anything that has **mass** and takes up **space (volume)**. Matter can shift between different [[States of Matter]] based on thermal energy (temperature) and pressure, noted down in [[Changes of Matter]], or transform entirely through [[Chemical Reactions]].


> [!ABSTRACT] Absolute Zero
>
> Absolute Zero is the phenomenon that happens when a system reaches $0^\circ K$ or $-273.15^\circ\text{C}$. In this state:
>
> - The atoms freeze completely in place, there is no molecular motion.
> - As we noted before, atoms lose their individual identities and clump together into a single quantum atom. They start acting like one giant particle instead of millions of individual ones.
> - They will become superconductors (no electrical resistance), and fluids will become superfluids (no viscosity aka no fluid friction)

## Types of Changes

#### Physical Changes

A physical change alters the form, size, or state of a substance, but **does not change its chemical composition**. The molecules remain exactly the same. These types of changes are usually easy to reverse.

Some examples include melting ice, dissolving sugar into water, and crushing a soda can.

#### Chemical Changes

A chemical change is another word for a [[Chemical Reactions]]. These are typically impossible or extremely difficult to reverse without another chemical reaction.

Some examples include rusting iron, burning wood, baking a cake, etc.
