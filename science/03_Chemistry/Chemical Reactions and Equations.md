Chemical reactions are a process where chemical substances (reactants) get transformed into different chemical substances (products).

This transformation usually involves breaking existing bonds and forming new bonds.
They do not create any new atoms, though the chemical structure is just rearranged.

There are a few indicators to tell when a reaction is taking place:
- a new solid forming
- bubbles forming
- a color changing
