The pH scale describes how acidic/alkaline a substance is.

- A lower pH indicates that the substance is acidic. Meaning that the substance has a lot of free hydrogen atoms.
- A pH of around 7 indicates that the substance is neutral.
- A higher pH indicates that the substance is alkaline. Meaning that the substance has a lot of free hydroxides, which absorb hydrogen atoms.

1 pH = 1 power of hydrogen

So a pH from 5 to 3 isn't just slightly more acidic, it is 100x more acidic, and vice versa.
