Atoms are fundamental building blocks of everything you see.

Atoms contain three main subatomic particles:
- Proton (positive charge, found in the nucleus)
- Electron (negative charge, found around the nucleus)
- Neutron (no charge, found in the nucleus with protons)

Though atoms are naturally neutral, which means the number of protons and electrons are the same.

There are some characteristics these control:
- Atomic Number: number of protons
- Ion: If an atom gains/loses an electron, it gains a net charge, which turns it into an ion.
- Isotope: Atoms of the same atomic number can have different numbers of neutrons.

Protons and Neutrons and glued together with the [[Strong Nuclear Force]].
Electrons however, are not, meaning they can be lost/gained much more easily.
## Elements

Elements are pure substances, consisting of atoms with the same atomic number.
They are also represented by a unique chemical symbol.

Examples:
- $O$ -> Oxygen, with an atomic number of 8, meaning it has 8 protons.
- $Fe$ -> Iron, with an atomic number of 26, meaning it has 26 protons.
