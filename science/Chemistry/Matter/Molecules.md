Molecules are electrically neutral groups of [[Atoms]] held together by a chemical bond.
There are two types of molecules: homonuclear and heteronuclear.
- An example of homonuclear molecules would be $\text{O}_2$ and $\text{H}_2$, as they only contain the same element.
- An example of heteronuclear molecules would be $\text{CO}_2$ or $\text{CH}_4$ as it has multiple elements.
