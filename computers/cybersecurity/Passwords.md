Passwords are secret strings of characters, and potentially numbers and symbols, used to authenticate a user's identity and give access to digital systems and services.

Passwords are pretty much necessary when it comes to account-making systems, signing up, logging in, etc.

## Best Practices

- Keep your passwords at least 12 characters long.
- Passwords should be unique, complex and nonsensical
- Use a mix of uppercase lowercase letters, numbers, and especially symbols.
- Don't reuse your passwords, as you do not want your other accounts compromised if one does get hacked into.
- Don't share your passwords unless you absolutely need to, and even then you should change it immediately after that person is done using it.
- Consider using MFA, or passkeys if a service supports them, as they are usually far safer than just a password.

I would also highly recommend using a secure, private and reputable password manager, like Bitwarden, as they can store your passwords, and even generate random passwords that are almost impossible to guess.

And also, I'm pretty sure this is obvious to a lot of people already but, again, don't save your passwords or login credentials in general in a public computer.
